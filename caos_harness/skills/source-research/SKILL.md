# Source research

State the decision and what evidence would change it. Prefer current primary sources, record the source and access date, and distinguish observed facts, calculations, hypotheses and unknowns. A source that cannot be read is not verified. Report limitations and deliver the result before saving it to a knowledge base; follow that knowledge base's instructions when explicitly asked to save.
