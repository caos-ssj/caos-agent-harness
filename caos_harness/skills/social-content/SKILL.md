# Social content

Start from the audience, topic, tone and evidence the user has approved. Do not assume that an old audience or campaign remains current. Separate demonstrations from client results and mark illustrative examples as such. Do not invent customers, metrics or testimonials.

Propose the concept and the real material needed to show it. Obtain the user's approval of the concept before producing the final media. Check every factual claim against its source and report gaps. The user decides whether and when to publish.
